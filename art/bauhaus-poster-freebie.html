How it works

Seven small steps from template to poster. Everything works with a free Canva account.

  1. Create the right sizeIn Canva choose Create a design → Custom size and enter 1333 × 1999 px. That is exactly the size of the template.
  2. Upload the templateOpen Uploads, add the PNG you downloaded and drag it onto the page. Stretch it until it fills the whole page.
  3. Add your photosUpload your own pictures and drag them onto the page. They will land on top of the template at first.
  4. Send them to the backClick a photo, then choose Position → Backward (or To back) until it sits behind the template. Now it only shows through the see-through spots.
  5. Move and resizeDrag each photo so the part you like sits in its shape. Repeat for every spot. Empty spots show the page color, so fill them all.
  6. ExportClick Share → Download. Choose PDF Print if you see it, otherwise PNG in the highest quality.
  7. Order your printUpload the file to a print service like CEWE, pick Poster and a size in the 2:3 ratio. Add a frame if you like. A teak or white frame looks great with these warm colors.

Before you hit "order"

Which size should you print?

The templates are 1333 × 1999 px. That's a good size for a gift poster, just not for a wall-sized one.

Recommended20 × 30 cm

Sharp and crisp, about A4 size. Perfect for a frame on a shelf or a gallery wall.

Still okay30 × 45 cm

Looks good from a normal distance, a little softer up close.

Not recommended40 × 60 cm +

The photos will look soft. For big posters you'd need a larger template.

For the best result use your original photos, not screenshots or pictures sent through a chat app. If the print service has a different ratio than 2:3, let it crop the edges. The white border gives you some room.
